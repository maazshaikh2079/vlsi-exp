# MONOLITH EDITORIAL — Design System (`DESIGN.md`)

- **Design System Name:** Monolith Editorial
- **Design Style:** Monochrome Editorial Minimalism *(Swiss-Inspired Technical Minimalism)*
- **Reference Inspiration:** [benjdd.com](https://benjdd.com/)

---

## 1. Overview & Design Style Classification
- **Monochrome / Achromatic Palette:** Warm matte charcoal (`#1e1e1e`), pure black surfaces (`#000000`), mid-tone neutral borders (`#646464`), and soft off-white typography (`#f0f0f0`) in dark mode, with an inverted high-craft light paper mode (`#e0e0e0` / `#141414`).
- **Editorial Single-Column Rhythm:** A focused reading measure (`48rem` / `768px`) where content flows vertically with generous whitespace and hairline horizontal rules.
- **Technical-Humanist Typography Pairing:** Light-weight geometric sans-serif prose (`Figtree`, weight `300`) paired with crisp, utilitarian monospace (`IBM Plex Mono`) for PDF download buttons.
