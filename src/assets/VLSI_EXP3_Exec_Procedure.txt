### Step 1: Software Required
- Microwind (e.g., Microwind 2 / Microwind 3.1 / Microwind 3.5 Lite)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Step 2: Lambda-Based Design Rules Reference
- N-Well:
  - Minimum well size (r101): 12 lambda
  - Between wells (r102): 12 lambda
- Diffusion (N+ and P+):
  - Minimum diffusion width (r201): 4 lambda
  - Between two diffusions (r202): 4 lambda
- Polysilicon Gate:
  - Polysilicon width (r301): 2 lambda
  - Polysilicon gate extension over diffusion (r302): 2 lambda
- Contacts & Metal 1:
  - Contact width (r401): 2 lambda
  - Metal 1 width: 3 lambda / 4 lambda

------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Step 3: Step-by-Step Execution in Microwind

#### A) Designing NMOS Layout
1. Launch Microwind software.
2. Select the foundry technology file (`File -> Select Foundry -> default.rul` for 0.12um CMOS 6-metal layers).
3. Either draw manually using the Palette:
   - Draw an **N+ Diffusion** box (green) of width >= 4 lambda.
   - Draw a vertical **Polysilicon** strip (red) crossing the center of the N+ diffusion (width = 2 lambda) to form the Gate.
   - Place **Metal 1 / N+ Contacts** on the left (Source) and right (Drain) diffusion regions.
   OR click the **MOS Generator** icon in the Palette, select **nMOS**, set `W` and `L`, and click **Generate Device**.
4. Add Simulation Properties from the Palette:
   - Connect **VSS (Ground)** to the Source terminal.
   - Add a **Clock / Pulse** or DC voltage property to the Drain and Gate terminals.
   - Place a **Visible Node (Eye icon)** on the output/drain to monitor current/voltage.
5. Click **Design Rule Checker (DRC)** (`Analysis -> Design Rule Checker` or F2) at every stage to ensure zero layout violations.
6. Click **Run Simulation** (`Simulate -> Run Simulation`):
   - Select **MOS Characteristics** to view the $I_D$ vs. $V_{DS}$ and $I_D$ vs. $V_{GS}$ curves.
   - Click **Process -> 3D Process View** to inspect the 3D fabrication steps of the NMOS transistor.

#### B) Designing PMOS Layout
1. Start a new layout (`File -> New`).
2. Draw an **N-Well** region (minimum 12 lambda).
3. Inside the N-Well, draw a **P+ Diffusion** box, cross it with a **Polysilicon** gate strip, and add **Metal 1 / P+ Contacts** on Source and Drain (or use the **MOS Generator** with **pMOS** selected).
4. Add **VDD Supply** to the N-Well and Source, apply clock/input to the Gate, and run **DRC**.
5. Run **Simulation -> MOS Characteristics** and inspect **3D Process View**.

### Step 4: Detailed Beginner's UI & Component Location Guide (Microwind)

If you have never used **Microwind** before, here is exactly where every button, tool, and component is located on the screen:

1. **Where to Find the Palette (Your Main Toolbox):**
   - When you open **Microwind**, you will see a **black grid canvas** on the left/center and a floating vertical window on the **right side** called the **`Palette`**.
   - *(If you don't see the Palette window, click **`View` -> `Palette`** on the top menu bar.)*

2. **The Easiest Way to Get an NMOS or PMOS Device (Using the MOS Generator Button):**
   - Look at the **top row of icons inside the `Palette` window** (right below the title bar of the Palette):
     - **1st icon (Arrow):** Select / Move cursor.
     - **2nd icon (Scissors / X):** Delete / Cut tool.
     - **3rd icon (Filled Rectangle):** `Draw Box` tool (used for manual drawing).
     - **4th icon (MOSFET Symbol — looks like a transistor gate):** **`MOS Generator`**!
   - Click that **4th icon (`MOS Generator`)** in the Palette:
     - A popup window titled **"MOS Generator"** opens.
     - Under **MOS**, select **`nMOS`** (for NMOS) or **`pMOS`** (for PMOS).
     - Leave Width (`W`) and Length (`L`) at default (or enter `W = 1.20 um`, `L = 0.12 um`).
     - Click the **`Generate Device`** button, then move your mouse onto the black canvas and **left-click once** to drop the complete transistor (diffusion, polysilicon gate, source/drain metal contacts, and N-well if PMOS) onto the grid!

3. **How to Draw Manually Using the Palette Layers (Optional Alternative to MOS Generator):**
   - If you want to draw rectangles manually, click the **3rd icon (`Draw Box`)** at the top of the Palette, then click one of the colored layer bars in the middle of the Palette:
     - **`N-Well` (top green hatched bar):** Click it, then drag a large rectangle on the black canvas (required around PMOS devices).
     - **`Polysilicon` (Bright Red bar):** Click it, then drag a vertical red strip (`2 lambda` wide) for the transistor **Gate**.
     - **`N+ Diffusion` (Dark Green bar):** Click it, then drag a horizontal green rectangle across the red Polysilicon strip to create an **NMOS**.
     - **`P+ Diffusion` (Yellow-Green bar):** Click it, then drag a horizontal rectangle inside the N-Well across the red Polysilicon strip to create a **PMOS**.
     - **`Contact` icons (squares with an `X` right below the color bars):** Click `Metal/N+ Contact` or `Metal/P+ Contact` and click on the left (Source) and right (Drain) sides of the diffusion.

4. **Where to Get Power (`VDD`), Ground (`VSS`), Clock, and Output Probes:**
   - Scroll to the **bottom section of the `Palette` window** (below the colored layer bars). You will see a row of simulation property icons:
     - **`VDD Supply` (looks like a `+` sign or T-bar):** Click this icon -> click on the **N-Well** and Source of the PMOS (or Drain supply) on the canvas.
     - **`VSS Ground` (looks like a 3-line Ground triangle):** Click this icon -> click on the **Source (left contact)** of the NMOS on the canvas.
     - **`Clock` (looks like a square pulse wave `_|-|_`):** Click this icon -> click on the **red Polysilicon Gate** of your transistor -> a popup appears -> click **`Assign`**. Do the same on the **Drain (right contact)** if simulating Voltage vs. Time.
     - **`Visible Node` (looks like an `Eye` icon):** Click this icon -> click on the **Drain (right contact)** -> click **`Assign`**.

5. **How to Check Design Rules (DRC), Run Simulation, and View 3D Process:**
   - **DRC (Design Rule Checker):** On the top menu bar, click **`Analysis` -> `Design Rule Checker`** (or press **`F2`**). Look at the bottom status bar—it should say `No error`.
   - **MOS Characteristics Curve:** On the top menu bar, click **`Simulate` -> `MOS Characteristics`** (or click the transistor curve icon). A window opens showing the exact $I_D$ vs. $V_{DS}$ curve of your drawn transistor! You can toggle between **`nMOS`** and **`pMOS`** and between **`Vd, Id`** (Output) and **`Vg, Id`** (Transfer) right inside that window.
   - **3D Process View:** On the top menu bar, click **`Simulate` -> `Process Steps in 3D`** (or **`Analysis` -> `Process View in 3D`**) to see the 3D silicon cross-section!
