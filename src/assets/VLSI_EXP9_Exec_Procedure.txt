### Step 1: Software Required

------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Step 2: 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder Theory & Example Calculation
- A **4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder (RCA)** cascades four Full Adders (`FA0`, `FA1`, `FA2`, `FA3`) where the carry-out ($C_i$) of each stage ripples into the carry-in of the next stage:
  - Sum: $S_i = A_i \oplus B_i \oplus C_{i-1}$
  - Carry: $C_i = (A_i \cdot B_i) + (B_i \cdot C_{i-1}) + (C_{i-1} \cdot A_i)$
- **Example verified in Journal (`0101` + `1010` with `Cin = 0`):**
  - Stage 1 (`FA0`): `A0=1, B0=0, Cin=0` -> `S0 = 1, C0 = 0`
  - Stage 2 (`FA1`): `A1=0, B1=1, C0=0`  -> `S1 = 1, C1 = 0`
  - Stage 3 (`FA2`): `A2=1, B2=0, C1=0`  -> `S2 = 1, C2 = 0`
  - Stage 4 (`FA3`): `A3=0, B3=1, C2=0`  -> `S3 = 1, C3 = 0`
  - Final Output: `Sum (S3S2S1S0) = 1111`, `Cout (C3) = 0`

------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Step 3: Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

1. Open **DSCH** software.
2. Draw the **4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder** schematic by cascading four 1-bit Full Adder blocks:
   - Connect inputs `A0..A3`, `B0..B3`, and initial carry-in `Cin`.
   - Connect `Cout` of each Full Adder to `Cin` of the next stage.
   - Connect output probes/LEDs to `S0..S3` and final `Cout`.
3. Run the logic simulation in DSCH and verify addition for test vectors (e.g., `A = 0101`, `B = 1010` -> `Sum = 1111, Cout = 0`).
4. Save the schematic (`.sch`) and click **File -> Make Verilog File** to generate the `.v` netlist.
5. Open **Microwind** software.
6. Go to **Compile -> Compile Verilog File**, select the generated `.v` file, click **Compile**, and then click **Back to Editor** to generate the CMOS layout automatically.
7. Run **DRC (Design Rule Checker)** to confirm there are no layout errors.
8. Click **Run Simulation** to observe the timing waveforms of inputs `A`, `B`, `Cin` and outputs `S0..S3`, `Cout`.

### Step 4: Detailed Beginner's UI & Component Location Guide (DSCH & Microwind)

If you have never used **DSCH** or **Microwind** before, here is the exact click-by-click guide showing where to find the Full Adder / logic gates, inputs, and outputs:

#### Part A: Where to Get Components in DSCH for the 4-Bit Ripple Carry Adder
1. **Open DSCH** and look at the **`Symbol Library`** panel on the **right side** of the screen (`View -> Symbol Library`).
2. **Two Ways to Place the 4 Full Adders in DSCH:**
   - **Shortcut Method (Using Pre-Built Full Adder Symbol in DSCH):**
     - Look at the tabs at the top of the right-side **`Symbol Library`** window (`Basic`, `Advanced` / `Arith`).
     - Click the **`Advanced`** tab (or look in the middle/lower rows of the `Symbol Library` for the **`Half / Full Adder` (`FA` / `Adder`)** block icon).
     - Click the **`Full Adder`** symbol and click **4 times** in a horizontal row on the canvas to place 4 Full Adders (`FA0`, `FA1`, `FA2`, `FA3`)!
   - **Gate-Level Method (Using XOR, AND, OR Gates from the `Basic` Tab):**
     - In the **`Basic` tab** of the `Symbol Library` (top rows):
       - Each 1-bit Full Adder uses **2 `XOR` gates** (for $S_i = A_i \oplus B_i \oplus C_{i-1}$), **2 `AND` gates**, and **1 `OR` gate** (for $C_i = (A_i \cdot B_i) + C_{i-1} \cdot (A_i \oplus B_i)$).
       - Tip: Once you wire **one** 1-bit Full Adder on the canvas, drag a selection box around it with the **Arrow cursor**, press **`Ctrl + C`** (Copy), and press **`Ctrl + V`** (Paste) **3 times** to create all 4 stages in seconds!
3. **Where to Get Inputs (`A0..A3`, `B0..B3`, `Cin`) and Outputs (`S0..S3`, `Cout`):**
   - In the **bottom section of the `Basic` tab** in the `Symbol Library`:
     - Click the **`Button` (Square Switch)** icon and place **9 Buttons**: four for `A0, A1, A2, A3`, four for `B0, B1, B2, B3`, and one for `Cin` (double-click each Button to rename it).
     - Click the **`Light / LED` (Round Bulb)** icon and place **5 LEDs**: four for sums `S0, S1, S2, S3` and one for final carry `Cout` (double-click each LED to rename it).
4. **How to Connect the Ripple Carry Chain:**
   - Click the **`Add a Line` (Wire / Pencil)** icon on the **top toolbar** (`Ctrl + L`).
   - Wire `Cin` into stage 0 (`FA0`).
   - Wire the Carry-Out (`Cout` / `C0`) of `FA0` into the Carry-In (`Cin`) of `FA1`.
   - Wire `C1` of `FA1` into `Cin` of `FA2`, and `C2` of `FA2` into `Cin` of `FA3`.
   - Wire the final Carry-Out (`C3`) of `FA3` to the `Cout` LED.
5. **Simulate in DSCH & Generate Verilog:**
   - Click **`Simulate` -> `Start Simulation`** (or the **Running Man / Play** icon on the top toolbar).
   - Set buttons `A3..A0 = 0101` (click `A2` and `A0` ON) and `B3..B0 = 1010` (click `B3` and `B1` ON) with `Cin = 0` -> verify that all four sum LEDs `S3..S0` glow Red (`1111`) and `Cout` is `0`!
   - Click **`Simulate` -> `Stop Simulation`**, then click **`File` -> `Make Verilog File`** -> click **`OK`**.

#### Part B: Automatic Layout & Simulation in Microwind
1. Open **Microwind**.
2. On the top menu bar, click **`Compile` -> `Compile Verilog File`** -> select the `.v` file saved from DSCH -> click **`Open`**.
3. In the **"Verilog File"** window, click **`Compile`** (bottom-right) -> then click **`Back to Editor`**.
4. Microwind automatically generates the complete 4-bit Ripple Carry Adder CMOS layout!
5. Press **`F2`** (`Analysis -> Design Rule Checker`) to verify zero errors.
6. Click **`Simulate` -> `Run Simulation`** (or the **Running Man / Play** icon on the top toolbar) to view the multi-bit adder timing waveforms!
