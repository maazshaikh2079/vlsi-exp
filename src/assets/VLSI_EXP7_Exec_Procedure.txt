VLSI EXPERIMENT 7 — EXECUTION PROCEDURE
Title: To design the layout of the D latch using micro-wind.

### Step 1: Software Required
- DSCH (Digital Schematic Editor)
- Microwind (Layout Editor & Simulator)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Step 2: Circuit & Theory Overview
- A **D Latch (Transparent Latch)** stores 1 bit of information.
- When `CLK` (Enable) is HIGH (`1`), output `Q` follows the data input `D`.
- When `CLK` falls to LOW (`0`), the last state of `D` is latched and held at `Q`.
- It is constructed from an SR latch by feeding `D` to `S` and `~D` (via an inverter) to `R`, gated by `CLK`.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Step 3: Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

#### A) Schematic & Verilog Generation in DSCH
1. Open **DSCH** software.
2. Draw the D Latch schematic (4 NAND gates + 1 NOT gate, or transmission-gate CMOS D-latch) with inputs `D` and `CLK` (or `EN`) and outputs `Q` and `~Q`.
3. Run the logic simulation in DSCH to verify that `Q` tracks `D` when `CLK = 1` and holds its value when `CLK = 0`.
4. Save the circuit and click **File -> Make Verilog File** to export the `.v` file.

#### B) Layout & Waveform Verification in Microwind
1. Start **Microwind** software for layout.
2. Compile the Verilog file (`Compile -> Compile Verilog File` -> `Compile` -> `Back to Editor`) or draw the D Latch CMOS layout following the lambda design rules.
3. Click **DRC (Design Rule Checker)** at every stage of design to ensure zero violations.
4. Save the layout and run a final **DRC**.
5. Click **Run Simulation** to observe the `D`, `CLK`, `Q`, and `~Q` voltage-time waveforms.
6. Record the layout and output waveforms observed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Step 4: Detailed Beginner's UI & Component Location Guide (DSCH & Microwind)

If you have never used **DSCH** or **Microwind** before, here is the exact click-by-click guide showing where to get every component for the **D Latch**:

#### Part A: Where to Get Components & How to Wire in DSCH
1. **Open DSCH** and locate the **`Symbol Library`** window on the **right side** (if hidden, click **`View` -> `Symbol Library`** on the top menu). Make sure the **`Basic`** tab is selected.
2. **Where to Get the Logic Gates (`NOT` Inverter & `2-Input NAND` Gates):**
   - Look at the **top rows of the `Basic` tab** in the `Symbol Library`:
     - **Inverter (`NOT` gate — triangle with bubble):** Click it once and place **1 Inverter** near the bottom-left of the canvas (this creates `~D` from `D`).
     - **`2-Input NAND` gate (D-shape with bubble):** Click it and place **4 NAND gates** on the canvas:
       - Two steering NAND gates in the middle-left column (`NAND1` at top, `NAND2` at bottom).
       - Two cross-coupled latch NAND gates in the middle-right column (`NAND3` at top, `NAND4` at bottom).
3. **Where to Get Inputs (`D`, `CLK`) and Outputs (`Q`, `~Q`):**
   - Look at the **bottom section of the `Basic` tab** in the `Symbol Library`:
     - **`Button` (Square Switch icon):** Click it and place **1 Button** at the top-left for Data input **`D`** (double-click the Button after placing it, type **`D`** in the Name box, and click `OK`).
     - **`Clock` (Square Pulse Wave `_|-|_` icon):** Click it and place **1 Clock** below `D` for the Clock/Enable input **`CLK`** (double-click it, type **`CLK`** in the Name box, and click `OK`).
     - **`Light / LED` (Round Bulb icon):** Click it and place **2 LEDs** on the far right: top LED named **`Q`** and bottom LED named **`~Q`**.
4. **How to Wire the D Latch:**
   - Click the **`Add a Line` (Wire / Pencil)** icon on the **top toolbar** (or press **`Ctrl + L`**):
     - Connect Data Button **`D`** to the top input of `NAND1`, AND also branch a wire from **`D`** into the input of the **`NOT` (Inverter)** gate.
     - Connect the output of the **`NOT` (Inverter)** gate to the bottom input of `NAND2`.
     - Connect **`CLK`** to both the bottom input of `NAND1` and the top input of `NAND2`.
     - Connect `NAND1` output to the top input of `NAND3`, and `NAND2` output to the bottom input of `NAND4`.
     - Cross-couple the latch: wire `NAND3` output (`Q`) to the top input of `NAND4`, and wire `NAND4` output (`~Q`) to the bottom input of `NAND3`.
     - Connect `NAND3` output to LED **`Q`** and `NAND4` output to LED **`~Q`**.
5. **Simulate in DSCH & Generate Verilog:**
   - Click **`Simulate` -> `Start Simulation`** (or the **Running Man / Play** icon on the top toolbar). Toggle the **`D`** button while `CLK` is high (`1`) and observe `Q` follow `D`!
   - Click **`Simulate` -> `Stop Simulation`**, then click **`File` -> `Make Verilog File`** -> click **`OK`**.

#### Part B: Converting to Layout & Simulating in Microwind
1. Open **Microwind**.
2. Click **`Compile` -> `Compile Verilog File`** on the top menu -> select your saved `.v` file from DSCH -> click **`Open`**.
3. In the **"Verilog File"** window, click **`Compile`** (bottom-right) -> then click **`Back to Editor`**.
4. The complete D Latch CMOS layout is automatically drawn on the canvas!
5. Press **`F2`** (`Analysis -> Design Rule Checker`) to confirm `No error`.
6. Click **`Simulate` -> `Run Simulation`** (or the **Running Man / Play** icon on the top toolbar) to view the `D`, `CLK`, `Q`, and `~Q` waveforms!
